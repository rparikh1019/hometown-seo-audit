Key Findings

1. Google can't find most of your pages Critical

A search for site:hometowncommunities.com/community/ returns only 2 pages. Your other communities are invisible to search engines.

Think of it like having 10 stores but only 2 listed in the phone book.
2. Duplicate URLs competing against each other Critical

Posada Del Sol exists at two different URLs. Google indexed both separately, so they're competing against each other instead of combining strength.

3. Missing meta descriptions Critical

Your pages have no meta descriptions. Google has to guess what your page is about, and it usually guesses wrong.

4. Technical errors on every page High

51 console errors detected, including 5+ broken images and JavaScript failures. This signals to Google that the site isn't well-maintained.

5. Third-party sites outrank you for your own properties High

TripAdvisor, Campspot, and Facebook pages about your communities rank higher than your actual website.

1. Indexing

Only 2 of your community pages are in Google's index Query: site:hometowncommunities.com/community/ returns just 2 URLs, both for the same property.

Why this happens:

  • No community-specific sitemap exists
  • Community pages aren't in any existing sitemap
  • Pages may not be linked from crawlable locations

2. Duplicate Content

Two URLs exist for Posada Del Sol:

/community/posada-del-sol/
/community/posada-del-sol-mhp-llc/

The first redirects to the second, but Google indexed both. They're competing against each other in rankings.
