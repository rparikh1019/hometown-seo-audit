The 5 Issues Hurting Your Business

Critical Issue 1: Google Can't Find Your Pages

What's happening: Out of all your community pages, Google only knows about 2 of them.

Business impact: If someone searches for one of your other communities, they won't find you. That's lost leads and lost revenue.

Analogy: You have 10 stores, but only 2 are listed in the phone book.

Critical Issue 2: Duplicate Listings

What's happening: Posada Del Sol has two different web addresses. Google sees these as competing pages.

Business impact: Instead of one strong listing, you have two weak ones fighting each other.

Analogy: Two billboards for the same store with different phone numbers. Customers get confused.

Critical Issue 3: Missing Page Descriptions

What's happening: Your pages don't have the short descriptions Google uses to understand your content.

Business impact: Google has to guess what your page is about. It often guesses wrong.

Analogy: A business card with your name but no description of what you do.

High Issue 4: Technical Problems

What's happening: Your pages have broken images, missing files, and 51 background errors.

Business impact: Google thinks your site isn't well-maintained, so it ranks you lower.

Analogy: A storefront with flickering lights and broken displays.

High Issue 5: Others Rank for YOUR Brand

What's happening: TripAdvisor, Campspot, and Facebook pages for your communities rank higher than your own website.

Business impact: Third-party sites control your narrative and get your traffic.

Analogy: Yelp reviews show up before your website. You don't control what they say.

1. Indexing Issues

Severe Under-Indexation: Only 2 community pages are indexed by Google.

Evidence

Query: site:hometowncommunities.com/community/ returns only 2 results.

Root Cause

  • page-sitemap.xml contains only 12 generic pages
  • property-sitemap.xml contains lot listings, not community pages
  • No dedicated community sitemap exists
  • Community pages are not discoverable by crawlers

Phase 1: Critical Fixes (Week 1-2)

1.1 Fix Indexing Issues

  • Create dedicated community-sitemap.xml containing all community pages
  • Add sitemap to sitemap_index.xml
  • Submit new sitemap to Google Search Console
  • Request indexing for each community page
  • Verify robots.txt allows crawling of /community/ path

1.2 Resolve Duplicate Content

  • Audit all community pages for duplicate URLs
  • Ensure 301 redirects are in place for all variations
  • Submit URL removal requests for duplicate indexed pages
  • Verify canonical tags on all community pages

1.3 Fix Broken Resources

  • Identify all 404 errors on community pages
  • Re-upload or remove references to missing images
  • Fix GSAP ScrollTrigger dependency loading
  • Re-test pages for console errors

Phase 2: On-Page Optimization (Week 2-4)

2.1 Meta Tag Implementation

  • Write unique title tags for each community page
    Format: [Community Name] | [Type] in [City], [State]
  • Write unique meta descriptions (150-160 characters)
    Include: community type, location, key amenity, CTA
  • Update robots meta to index, follow
  • Optimize H1 tags with location keywords

2.2 Schema Markup Enhancement

  • Implement LocalBusiness or RVPark schema on each community page
  • Add Place schema with geo coordinates
  • Add Offer schema for available homes
  • Implement FAQPage schema for common questions
  • Test all schema with Google Rich Results Test

2.3 Content Optimization

  • Add location keywords naturally throughout content
  • Ensure each community page has 500+ words of unique content
  • Add internal links to related communities and city pages
  • Optimize image alt text with descriptive keywords

Phase 3: Local SEO (Week 4-6)

3.1 Google Business Profile Setup

  • Create or claim Google Business Profile for each community
  • Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) matches website exactly
  • Add photos, hours, amenities, and services
  • Link GBP to corresponding website page
  • Set up review request process

3.2 Directory Listings

  • Create/claim listings on MHVillage, MHBay, 55places.com
  • Claim existing TripAdvisor and Campspot listings
  • Ensure NAP consistency across all directories

3.3 City Landing Pages

  • Create optimized landing pages for each city served
  • Include list of communities in that city
  • Add local content (neighborhood info, nearby attractions)

Phase 4: Ongoing Optimization (Month 2+)

4.1 Content Development

  • Create FAQ content for each community
  • Develop neighborhood guides
  • Write blog posts targeting long-tail keywords
  • Add resident testimonials with Review schema

4.2 Link Building

  • Reach out to local chambers of commerce
  • Get listed in local business directories
  • Pursue local news/PR opportunities

4.3 Monitoring & Reporting

  • Set up Google Search Console monitoring
  • Track keyword rankings weekly
  • Monitor indexing status monthly
  • Quarterly technical audits
